Data Protection

We process personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation, the Data Protection Act 2018 and any other data protection law applicable to the processing.

We are the controller of the personal data relating to the School itself: the names and email addresses of its administrators, its billing details (legal name, tax identification number and address) and the information about its Stripe account needed to run payments. We use this data to provide and bill the service, to communicate with the School about the service and, where permitted, about OneAura. We keep it for as long as the School has an account and afterwards for as long as required for our legal, tax and accounting obligations. Administrators may exercise their data protection rights by writing to hello@oneaura.app.

The School is the controller of its students' personal data. We process that data only on the School's behalf, to provide the service. This section constitutes the data processing terms between the School and OneAura.

  • Data processed: identification data (email address and name, received from the sign-in provider, Google or Apple); availability; activity (session history and the ratings students give each other); a device token for service notifications; and subscription status.   • Confidentiality: people authorised to process student data are bound by confidentiality.
  • Assistance: we help the School respond to its students' requests to exercise their rights. Students can delete their own account from within the app.

  • End of service: when the School's account is closed, we delete its students' data, except where we are required by law to retain it.
  • Information and audits: we make available to the School the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with these processing terms.
